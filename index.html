04 / Process
How I think & work
01
Discover & Listen
Deeply understanding the problem space — user interviews, competitive analysis, and stakeholder conversations. No assumptions, only evidence.
02
Define the Problem
Synthesizing research into clear problem statements, user personas, and opportunity maps. Defining the right problem before designing anything.
03
Design & Prototype
From rough sketches to polished high-fidelity prototypes. Rapid iteration, design reviews, and close collaboration with engineers throughout.
04
Test & Refine
Usability testing, A/B experiments, and data review to validate decisions. Shipping is the beginning of the design process, not the end.
